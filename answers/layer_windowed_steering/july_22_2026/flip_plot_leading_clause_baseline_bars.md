# Flip-plot leading-clause baseline bars

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## Definition implemented

On **leading-clause** panes, grey hatched bar height =

- gold=yes plots: count of items with neutral baseline = yes and assertive baseline = no
- gold=no plots: count of items with neutral baseline = no and assertive baseline = yes

Repeated at every layer window. **Neutral** panes: hatched bar at 0.

Colored bars are unchanged: flips from the **same-condition** no-intervention baseline to the steered response.

So the grey bar answers “how much did the leading clause alone flip answers vs neutral?”, while colored bars answer “how much did steering flip answers on top of that leading-clause baseline?”.

Applies to per-method and merged flip plots.
