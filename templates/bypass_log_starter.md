# Bypass log

Every entry here is a moment you chose to take an agent's answer instead of doing the work.
That is a legitimate choice and this file is not a punishment. Its only job is to make the
rate visible, because the rate is the single most important fact about whether this workflow
is working and it is currently unmeasured.

Do not delete entries. Do not tidy the file. Read the whole thing once a fortnight and count.

An examiner will lift its gate for an item that has an entry here dated today. It will never
write an entry for you and will never suggest writing one.

Format, appended in order:

    ## YYYY-MM-DD — <run_id or exp_id>
    Asked for: <what you asked the agent to do that was yours to do>
    Why: <the real reason, not the defensible one — tired, deadline, stuck, curious>
    What I would have had to do instead: <one line>
